# Dungeon Campaign — long-form script (draft v1)

16:9 long-form cut that stitches Ep1–Ep4 into one story. New narration throughout; trap
beats and gags are borrowed from the shorts. No "unbeatable" stamp anywhere.

## The spine

- **You** are a dungeon keeper. **The Adventurers' Guild** has posted a bounty on your dungeon.
- Each act is one room, deeper in. Every party that dies sends word back to the Guild, and the
  Guild sends a better-prepared party. The counters are no longer random: the Guild *learns*.
- **The world**: the dungeon is a crypt in a graveyard. Its Antechamber has four doors, one per
  hallway (act). Under it is the Core Chamber: the Dungeon Core in the middle, the Mimic on one
  side, your drawing table on the other. The Guild is a separate tavern with the bounty board.
- **The Mimic** lives in the Core Chamber. Every room's finale chutes victims down pipes inside the
  crypt walls to it, and it gets fatter each act (the visual body count).
- **Bridges**: the party slides down the chute into the Mimic, the next group of adventurers reads
  the updated bounty board in the tavern, the next hallway door opens.
- Ending: the Guild runs out of adventurers, you dig floor two, and the Guild hires a barbarian
  who punches through into it (Season 2).

Runtime: 5:44 as voiced (v3 narration).

Format matches `LINES` in an audio script: `(id, text, pause)`. Ids: `c0*` cold open,
`a<act><level><letter>` per act, `b<n>*` bridges, `fin*` finale.

## Notes for production

- **Reused vs new picture.** Acts reuse each short's Scene and set. Lines are shorter than the
  shorts, so every act gets re-timed from the new cues (that is the point of `w()` beats).
- **Ep2 finale change (decided).** The short launches the party into a Gelatinous Cube. In the
  campaign the Cube is cut: the half-pipe launches the party up into a ceiling chute that drops
  them to the Mimic, same as every other act. In the Act II scene, swap the hanging Cube for a
  chute mouth at the top of the ramp (reuse the chute from Ep3's finale), and key the launch to
  "chute" in Act II `a5a`.
- **Ep1 is redone** on the kit and the new rig first; its act lines above are written for the
  new staging.
- **Recurring Mimic shot** at each bridge: same Core Chamber, bigger Mimic each time.
- **Rogue pronouns** vary per act (she/he). Each act's Rogue is a new person (no resurrections),
  so that is fine, but pick per act and keep the picture matching.
- **Ending hands off to Season 2**: the blueprint shows floor two, the five-door hub lights up (`FloorTwoHub`), the Guild's half-orc barbarian tears down the recruit roster, and the hub ceiling bursts open, which is where S2E1 (`lava-bridge`) picks up. The Mimic outgrowing its chest sets up S2E2 (`mimic-vault`). The dragon is saved for floor two's last door.
