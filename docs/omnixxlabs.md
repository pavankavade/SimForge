this before you pick, title or script a video for this channel. Update the tables whenever you
check the channel again.

- **What it hosts:** the `src/shelter` "How an X becomes a Y" 3D shorts, plus a few survival
  what-ifs (`src/whatif`) and the legacy siege-tower short.

## What the numbers say

The three hits (58K, 23K, 19K) share four things. The flops (under 3K) miss at least two.

1. **It starts in your own home or body.** A closet, a hole in your yard, *you* trapped
   underground. The viewer pictures themselves in it. The candle, cave, igloo and siege tower are
   someone else's problem.
2. **A real, modern fear.** Tornadoes, nuclear war, being buried alive. Historical or quaint
   threats (sieges, ships at night, WW1) do not pull, even when the build is good.
3. **A mundane object turned into an extreme structure.** "How a Closet Becomes a Tornado
   Shelter" is the strongest title so far: an everyday thing, a big disaster word, and a promise
   of a transformation. "Evolution of …" titles all did poorly on this channel.
4. **Real engineering, one step per level, with one myth busted.** The tornado short tested
   debris walls, anchors and doors and busted "open a window". Viewers come for the "huh, I
   didn't know that".

Other observations:

- **Survival what-ifs work when they are about *you*** ("What If You Were Trapped …", 23K). A
  famous true story told in third person ("Trapped in a Flooding Cave … Elon Musk", 1.5K) did
  not, even with a celebrity in the title. Put the cave-rescue-style stories in the second person.
- **Emoji in titles** did not help (the lighthouse has two and is near the bottom).
- **Hashtags in titles** (`#3danimation`) did not help either.
- Subscribers tripled in three weeks on the back of the tornado short. The next upload should
  follow the hit format closely while that traffic is warm.
- **The channel About text is still a stale Family Guy clip-channel blurb** ("Giggity Gold …").
  It contradicts the channel's content. The user should replace it in YouTube Studio with one line
  about the shorts (for example, "3D shorts: how everyday things become survival shelters").

## Rules for the next videos

- Title: `How a <everyday object in your home> Becomes a <disaster> Shelter` or
  `What If You Were <trapped/caught> in <disaster>?`. No emoji or hashtags in the title.
- Line one of the script puts the viewer in their own home or body with the disaster arriving.
- Five or six levels of real engineering, each fixing the last failure, with one myth busted.
- Every fact checked against an official source (FEMA, NWS, NOAA, Red Cross) before voicing.
  Retell in our own words, never copy a reference video (see the `retell-not-copy` rule).
- Finish on the stamp, then the comment hook (AGENTS.md §10).
