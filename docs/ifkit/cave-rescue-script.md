# Cave Rescue: "18 days inside a mountain" (IF Kit, 9:16 short)

Our retelling of the 2018 Tham Luang cave rescue in Thailand, told to "you", one of the boys.
Our own words; the same true facts as the news record. Composition `CaveRescue`
(`src/whatif/cave-rescue`), audio `scripts/cave-rescue-audio.py`, ~1:43.

## Facts we use

- 23 June 2018: the Wild Boars, twelve boys aged 11–16 and their 25-year-old assistant coach,
  go into Tham Luang Nang Non after football practice. Early monsoon rain floods the cave behind
  them. Their bikes are left at the entrance.
- They retreat about 4 km in, to a muddy bank above the water. No food; they drink water
  dripping from the rock. The coach has them sit still and meditate to stay calm and save energy.
- Pumps drain the cave around the clock; divers fight strong currents in zero-visibility water.
- 2 July (day 10): British cave divers Rick Stanton and John Volanthen surface in the pool below
  the bank and find all thirteen alive ("How many of you?" "Thirteen." "Brilliant.").
- The way out is hours of diving through flooded, sometimes very tight passages, in darkness.
  Oxygen in the chamber falls.
- 6 July (day 14): former Thai Navy SEAL Saman Kunan dies on his way back from laying air tanks
  along the route.
- The boys are sedated (ketamine-based anaesthesia), given full-face masks, wrapped in
  stretchers and carried out by divers: four on 8 July, four on 9 July, four and the coach on
  10 July (day 18).
- Elon Musk's team built a kid-sized submarine from rocket parts ("Wild Boar") and brought it to
  the cave. Narongsak Osottanakorn, head of the operation, called it not practical; it was never
  used, and Musk left it in Thailand.
- Afterwards British caver Vern Unsworth, who helped the rescue, called the sub a PR stunt on CNN.
  Musk called him "pedo guy" on Twitter, deleted the tweets and apologised. Unsworth sued; on
  6 December 2019 a Los Angeles federal jury found Musk not liable for defamation.

Sources: TechCrunch / SBS on the mini-sub ("not practical"), BuzzFeed News / AP on the verdict, AFP timeline (Inquirer), History.com "Thai soccer team rescued from cave", the medics'
letter in the New England Journal of Medicine as reported by AP (ketamine, masks), Shepton Mallet
Caving Club summary (divers' arrival).

## Look

One world, cut open like an ant farm: a limestone mountain with jungle on top, the cave winding
down through it on three levels (the 4 km squeezed into ~90 m), three U-bends that flood in turn,
the pool and the muddy ledge at the bottom. Realistic humans (the paladin base human): seven of
the boys and the coach in football kit, three divers in black wetsuits with helmets, cap lamps,
masks, side tanks and fins. The other boys are background figures on the ledge.
