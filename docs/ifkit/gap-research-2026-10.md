The 4–5 formats that work: **a stat × N**, **a hidden number over every head**, **time perception**,
**redo with your memories**, and **day 1 → day N doubling**. The stories are grounded: one teenager,
school, a sister in danger.

## What is saturated (skip)

- **IQ:** 500, 1000, 10,000 and infinite IQ, at school, in prison and with $0. Dozens of copies, most under 2K views.
- **Reaction time, reflexes, speed, punches, kicks and arms × N.**
- **Hearing, eyesight and smell 100x.** Each already has 5–8 identical AI copies (under 1.2K views).
- **Number-over-head:** lifespan, IQ, lie count, likes you, hates you, crushes, bank balance, secrets,
  betrayal, talent and "how many times you'll see someone again".
- **Seeing 3, 5 or 10 seconds into the future, rewinding 10 seconds, pausing everyone, never sleeping, no pain, luck × 100, never missing a shot.**
- **Classic powers:** teleport, fly, invisible, shrink, clone, walk through walls, read minds and control gravity.

Every premise in this genre is copied word for word by 5–10 channels within days. A premise is never
an advantage for long. Execution is, along with getting there first with a fresh rule.

## Plan A (chosen): proven premises with a real catch

While the channel grows, stay inside the proven formats. Every variable inside them is already
cloned (jump 10x, memory 100x, learn 100x, IQ doubling, strength doubling, waking up at 4/7/16 with
memories), so the gap is the **angle**, not the premise.

Evidence: FunNotSoFun (7 videos) got 25K with "Your Muscles Got 10x Stronger. Your Bones Didn't.",
189K with "Your Lies Weigh Nothing. Your Sister Carries Them." and 943K with "What If Your Money Grew
10x Every Day?". The few channels that tried a catch on the biggest hits did it badly (7–93 views),
so in practice the catch versions are open.

| Our title | Parent hit | Catch-twist attempts so far | The real catch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Your Reaction Time Got 10x Faster. Your Body Didn't. | 4.6M (Ifverse) | 7 and 29 views | You see the ball coming for a full second, but your muscles still move at normal speed. |
| You're 10x Faster. The Air Isn't. | 849K (IFOLOGY) | none | Wind like a hurricane, friction burning your shoes, and you can't turn corners. |
| You Can Stop Time. The Air Stops Too. | 620K (IFOLOGY) | 93 views | Air is a wall, there's no light reaching your eyes and no breath. |
| You Heal 100x Faster. You're Starving. | 387K (If Then Tales) | none | Healing burns calories: a broken arm costs a week of meals in one minute. |
| You Grow 10x Bigger. Your Heart Doesn't. | 242K (Ifinite) | none | The square-cube law: you faint, your knees fail, you overheat. |

Two growth formats to follow up with:

- **"We tested your plans"** (124K): a sequel built from viewers' comments on a hit episode.
- **Cost transfer** (189K): the power is free for you, and someone you love pays for it.

## Plan B, for later: gaps (searched: zero or near-zero results in this genre)

| Premise | Closest existing | Why it suits IF Kit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What if you could see when anything will break?** (a countdown on every object) | "close to breaking" (emotional, 41 views) | Very visual on our 3D engine. The ladder, the bike chain, the elevator cable and finally a bridge or roller coaster. Real engineering physics. A hero arc. |
| **What if your shadow moved 10 seconds before you?** | 5 views, one horror short | A brand-new rule, easy to read at phone size, and cheap for us (the actor's own track shifted 300 frames). The catch: everyone can see it, so people read your next move. |
| **What if you could see the odds of everything?** (a % over every action) | "Control your luck" 48K, a different idea | The HUD-number format without a copy. The ladder "73% slip". The story turns when he stops trusting the numbers. |
| **What if you could replay the last hour of any place?** (ghosts of the past) | Touching objects 102 views; a Chinese drama "hear antiques talk" 1.2M proves demand | A detective story (Grandma's missing ring, who broke the window). Ghost replays are almost free for us, because every actor is a pure function of frame. |
| **What if you could see everyone's near misses?** (a counter of how many times each person almost died) | Nothing | It flips the death-timer hit (1.6M) into something hopeful: the grandpa with 212 and the kid with 0. A strong emotional twist. |
| **What if you could see where every lost thing is?** | Nothing in the genre | Wholesome and kid-friendly. A lost-and-found hero who escalates to a lost dog, then a lost person. |

## Our edge

Competitors are mostly 2D stickman/AI art and ignore physics. IF Kit can own **"the same premises,
but the physics is real"** (Teleport's momentum catch), told as a grounded story in 3D. Pick gap
premises where the rule has a concrete, physical, visual consequence.

## Recommendation

1. **See when anything will break.** It has the strongest visuals, real physics and a clear climax.
2. **Shadow 10 seconds ahead.** The freshest rule and the cheapest to make.
3. **Replay the last hour of any place.** A detective format that plays to our engine.
