# 69 Days Under a Mountain (draft script v1)

IF Kit short, 9:16, target 80–90 s. Our own retelling of the 2010 San José mine rescue (Chile),
second person, told with the realistic base human as the miners.

**Look:** one world, cut open like an ant farm. The mountain's face stands toward the camera and
everything underground is carved into it: the shelter at the bottom, the ramp zig-zagging down to
it with its road, lights and a truck, the ventilation shaft, the boreholes and the rescue shaft.
The camp, the rigs and the crowd stand on top. Crisp sunlit rock, no depth of field. The camera
stays in that one world: it whips out to the whole mountain and back in, and rides down the holes
with the drill bits, the supply tube and the capsule.

**Cast:** you (hero miner, close-ups), the foreman, four miners around you and two rescuers at the
camp are the realistic human. The rest of the 33 are light background figures at the far end of
the shelter (never behind a face in a close-up), and the crowd at the camp is the same.

**Facts we lean on** (all public record): collapse on 5 Aug 2010, the shelter about 700 m down,
emergency food for about two days, ventilation shaft ladders never installed, rations of two spoons
of tuna, a sip of milk and a bite of cracker every 48 h, probe holes about 12 cm wide that missed
on old maps, breakthrough on day 17 (22 Aug), the red note "Estamos bien en el refugio los 33",
*palomas* (supply tubes), three rival drilling plans (Plan B broke through on 9 Oct, day 65), the
Fénix capsule (53 cm wide, sprung wheels, its own oxygen, an escape hatch underneath), the first
man up in sunglasses, all 33 out in about 22 hours, and foreman Luis Urzúa last.

## What the base human needs

- **Miner gear:** a hard hat with a lamp (and its beam), overalls (from the doublet layer, recoloured
  orange/blue with reflective bands), boots, gloves and sunglasses. All new slots next to the plate,
  so the paladin keeps working.
- **New poses:** sit (on a bench), eat (hand to mouth), look up, crouch, push-up, cheer/hug, and
  standing still inside the capsule. They are added to the animator so the game gets them too.
- **New props:** mountain cutaway set, shelter, surface camp, drill rig with a probe bit, paloma tube,
  three big rigs, Fénix capsule with a winch tower, floodlights.
