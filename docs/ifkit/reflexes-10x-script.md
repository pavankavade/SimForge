# What If You Could React 10x Faster… But Your Body Couldn't? (draft script v1)

IF Kit what-if, long-form, target 5–6 minutes (64 lines, about 5:30–6:00 at Teleport's pace).
It is built on the genre's biggest hit (Ifverse, "What If Your Reaction Time Was 10x Faster?", 4.6M),
plus the real catch nobody has executed: the brain speeds up, the nerves and muscles don't.

**Title options** (channel style "What If You Could ___… But ___?"):
1. What If You Could React 10x Faster… But Your Body Couldn't?
2. What If Your Reflexes Were 10x Faster… But Your Body Wasn't?
3. What If Your Brain Ran 10x Faster… But Your Body Didn't?

**Cast:** you (the kid from Teleport), your little sister Tess, Grandpa Teo (both from Teleport, so
IF Kit gets a recurring family), and Marcus, an eighth grader with the hardest throw in school.

**Thesis:** you can't be faster, only earlier. It is set up by the dodgeball and paid off by the ladder.

## Numbers behind the lines

- **Reaction time:** a simple visual reaction takes about 0.25 s. With the in-head part (seeing, recognising, deciding) 10x
  faster, what's left is nerve conduction (fast motor fibres about 90 m/s, about 200 mph; 1 m to the hand is about 0.01–0.02 s) plus
  electromechanical delay (muscle switch-on, about 0.03–0.05 s). The total is about 0.1 s, roughly 2.5x faster.
- **False starts:** World Athletics counts any reaction under 0.100 s as a false start.
- **Milk glass:** a fall from a 75 cm table takes 0.39 s, which is 3.9 felt seconds. You need 0.1 s plus about 0.25 s of hand
  movement, so you catch it. A normal person needs 0.25 s plus 0.25 s and misses.
- **Blink:** 0.1–0.15 s, so 1–1.5 felt seconds.
- **School:** a 6 h day feels like 60 h; a 50 min class feels like 8.3 h.
- **Slow pain:** C fibres carry it at about 0.5–2 m/s. Toe to brain is about 1.6 m, so about 1.1 s, or 11 felt seconds. The sharp
  first pain on A-delta fibres arrives in about 0.1 s.
- **Dodgeball:** 40 mph is 18 m/s, and 20 ft is 6 m, so the ball takes 0.33 s. A sidestep needs about 0.35 s of movement.
  - Watching the ball: you need 0.1 + 0.35 = 0.45 s but only have 0.33 s, so you're hit.
  - Watching the shoulder: the 0.25 s windup gives you 0.58 s, which is enough for you. A normal player needs 0.6 s and is
    still hit.
- **Ladder:** treated as an inverted pendulum, about 3 m with Grandpa. τ = √(2L/3g) = 0.45 s. From 1° it reaches 5° at
  1.04 s, 14° at 1.5 s and 30° at 1.85 s, and hits the ground at 2.34 s.
  - You: react 0.1 s plus a 1.4 s sprint, so you arrive at 1.5 s, at about 14°.
  - A normal kid: notices at 5° (1.04 s), reacts 0.25 s, sprints 1.4 s, and arrives at 2.69 s, which is 0.35 s after
    the ladder lands.
